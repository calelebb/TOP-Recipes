Vodka Pasta

Ingredients

  • Rigatoni noodles
  • Tomato Paste
  • Heavy Cream
  • Garlic
  • Shallot
  • Protein of your choice
  • Parmesean

Steps

  1. Start cooking the noodles
  2. Cook protein in the pan prior to the following steps to increase flavor
  3. Cook shallots and Garlic
  4. Add tomato paste and let simmer for a couple minutes
  5. Add Heavy Cream
  6. Add pasta water
  7. Add pasta into sauce and stir vigorously.
