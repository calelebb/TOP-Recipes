Chicken and Potatoes

Ingredients

  • Chicken
  • Golden Potatoes
  • Butter
  • Garlic
  • Herbs

Steps

  1. Cut Potatoes into Cubes
  2. Season chicken with chicken seasoning.
  3. Put Chicken and cubed potatoes into sheet pan
  4. Bake in the oven on 400F for 1 hour.
  5. Add butter on top of potatoes and chicken and let melt
